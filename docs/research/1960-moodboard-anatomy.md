# Moodboard anatomy for product, brand, and UI design

Research for Argo issue [#1960](https://github.com/milad-alizadeh/argo/issues/1960), completed on 2026-09-12.

## Research question

This report defines the visible anatomy of a professional moodboard for product, brand, and UI design. It also explains what each section communicates.

A moodboard is a collage that defines a design direction. It combines images, typography, color, swatches, and other references in one place. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) and [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) both describe this purpose.

The sources do not prescribe one universal template. They agree on a common core, but they allow the content to follow the project goal. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) calls textures, fonts, icons, and layouts optional. [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) says that the purpose and goal guide what belongs on the board.

The anatomy below is therefore a synthesis. It combines first-party moodboard guidance with first-party design-system foundations. Each recommendation states what evidence supports it.

## Recommended artifact anatomy

A complete direction needs one board frame with the following ten sections. The sections can overlap visually, but each one must remain identifiable.

| Section | Visible elements | What the section communicates | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Direction header | A distinct direction name, one-sentence premise, project goal, and three to five anchor words | The intended feeling, audience, and decision lens for every other reference | Figma starts with the project goal and descriptive anchor words. Milanote starts with a direction name and initial ideas. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) |
| 2. Hero reference | One dominant image or visual at the center or strongest focal point | The fastest summary of the direction and the visual idea that carries the most weight | Figma recommends a central hero image with supporting elements around it. Milanote recommends one key element that anchors the board. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) |
| 3. Image world | Curated photography, illustration, art, or object references; include subject matter, crop, depth, lighting, and treatment examples | The world that the product or brand inhabits, plus its emotional tone and image-making rules | Figma names inspiring images, illustrations, photography styles, and original photography. Milanote says that image style has the greatest impact on the mood. Canva lists photography, sketches, and illustrations. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) [Canva](https://www.canva.com/create/mood-boards/) |
| 4. Color behavior | A compact palette, a dominant-to-accent proportion example, and colors shown on representative surfaces or images | The emotional temperature, contrast, emphasis, and relationship between brand color and interface color | Adobe says that color expresses brand personality. Figma requires a defined palette and recommends balance across the board. Apple says that color can evoke a brand, provide continuity, and communicate status or feedback. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color) |
| 5. Typography | Display and text specimens, a font pairing, scale contrast, weight contrast, and one short phrase in the proposed voice | The personality of the words and the intended hierarchy between display, heading, and reading text | Adobe says that typography reflects tone of voice. Figma names font pairings as moodboard content. Apple says that type conveys hierarchy, important content, brand, and style. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) |
| 6. Composition and space | A layout reference, grid or alignment cue, density example, whitespace example, overlap or crop behavior, and the board's own composition | The rhythm, hierarchy, density, and spatial character that future screens or brand layouts must follow | Figma includes UI and layout references, asks for enough space, and calls for visual hierarchy. Milanote uses size and position to show importance and relationships. Atlassian treats spacing and grid as foundations of page layout. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations) |
| 7. Shape and graphic language | Geometric motifs, corner behavior, borders, line style, icon style, patterns, marks, and a logo when one exists | The repeated visual grammar that makes unrelated elements feel like one family | Canva lists logos, icons, sketches, illustrations, backgrounds, and textures. Atlassian lists iconography, logos, borders, and radius as distinct visual foundations. Its icon guidance links corners, curves, and line endings to brand expression. [Canva](https://www.canva.com/create/mood-boards/) [Atlassian foundations](https://atlassian.design/foundations) [Atlassian iconography](https://atlassian.design/foundations/iconography/) |
| 8. Material and surface | Texture or material swatches, background treatment, translucency, grain, shadow, depth, or flatness examples | How surfaces feel and how foreground, background, and layered content separate | Adobe recommends physical material samples, texture close-ups, and packaging prototypes. Apple defines material as a visual effect that creates depth, layers, and hierarchy. Atlassian says that surfaces and shadows create lift and can guide focus. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/elevation/) |
| 9. Product fragments | Two to four small, representative UI fragments, such as a button, card, navigation item, form field, chart, or empty state | Proof that the direction can become a usable product language, not only a collage aesthetic | Figma names UI design as moodboard content. Its examples use button shape, whitespace, dark mode, and UI patterns to connect brand feeling with product function. Atlassian lists color, spacing, grid, type, icons, elevation, borders, and radius as UI foundations. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations) |
| 10. Rationale and annotations | Short labels beside key choices, one sentence about each section's intent, source links, and a clear feedback area | Why each reference belongs, what to notice, and where reviewers can respond | Figma says that commentary adds meaning and strengthens the board as a presentation tool. Milanote asks for notes about the thought process and supports captions. Adobe recommends notes about the thinking behind board elements. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/ultimate-guide-mood-boards) |

## Details for each section

### 1. Direction header

The header needs enough context to judge the board. It does not need a full creative brief. Figma asks the maker to define the story and choose descriptive anchor words. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)

Use a distinct name for each direction. Milanote tells designers to create a separate board for each direction and give each board a name. [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

The name cannot carry the distinction by itself. The premise and anchor words must still make the intended character clear when the name is hidden. This is an inference from Figma's use of anchor words and Adobe's use of words that reinforce the theme. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board)

### 2. Hero reference

Give one image more visual weight than the rest. Figma recommends a hero image in the center. Milanote recommends changing size and position to show importance. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

The hero can show a place, object, person, artwork, material, or composition. It does not need to show the final product. Adobe says that references can come from outside the immediate industry. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board)

### 3. Image world

The section needs a curated set, not a general image dump. Adobe recommends a few key images, a clean focus, and removal of unrelated styles. Figma recommends 10 to 15 high-impact images for the whole board. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)

Show a repeatable treatment, such as soft natural light, direct flash, monochrome illustration, tight crops, or wide environmental views. This recommendation follows Canva's advice to vary image depth and Figma's call for a photography style. [Canva](https://www.canva.com/create/mood-boards/) [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)

Use original or primary references when possible. Figma recommends original photography. Adobe recommends sources from the place, period, or aesthetic that the direction represents. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/ultimate-guide-mood-boards)

### 4. Color behavior

Do not show isolated swatches only. Show how a dominant color, supporting colors, neutrals, and accents behave together. This is a synthesis of Figma's color balance guidance and Apple's guidance about emphasis, status, and consistent meaning. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color)

The moodboard records direction, not final color tokens. Milanote permits colors from existing brand assets or sampled images. Atlassian defines tokens as the later source of truth for interface decisions. [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/tokens/design-tokens/)

### 5. Typography

Show type in use, not only font names. Include a display phrase and a reading-size sample. Apple says that size, weight, and color create hierarchy. Milanote recommends font combinations, weights, and type treatments. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

Keep the number of typefaces small enough to preserve hierarchy. Apple warns that too many typefaces can obscure hierarchy and reduce consistency. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography)

### 6. Composition and space

The board must embody its proposed composition. A spacious direction needs visible breathing room. A dense direction needs controlled density and clear hierarchy. Figma says to balance color, leave enough space, and arrange elements in a readable flow. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)

Show at least one reference for layout behavior. Figma names layouts and UI design as possible moodboard elements. Atlassian states that grid positions content and spacing supports page layouts. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations)

### 7. Shape and graphic language

Show the direction's repeated geometry. Examples include rounded or sharp corners, organic or rectilinear shapes, line endings, border weight, and icon detail. Atlassian shows that these choices create cohesion and express a brand. [Atlassian iconography](https://atlassian.design/foundations/iconography/) [Atlassian radius](https://atlassian.design/foundations/radius/)

Include logos only when they are existing inputs or when identity is part of the direction. Adobe lists logos for brand moodboards. Canva lists logos and icons as common content. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Canva](https://www.canva.com/create/mood-boards/)

### 8. Material and surface

Material can be physical or digital. Physical examples include fabric, paper, paint, or packaging. Digital examples include grain, blur, translucency, flat color, and shadow. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/elevation/)

Use material to show hierarchy, not only decoration. Apple says that material separates foreground from background. Atlassian says that elevation guides focus through layers. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/elevation/)

### 9. Product fragments

Keep fragments small and representative. They must show how color, type, shape, space, and material meet in product UI. Figma's examples connect mood references to buttons, whitespace, dark mode, grids, and other UI patterns. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)

Do not turn the moodboard into several full screen designs. Figma defines the artifact as a collection of references that sets direction. Adobe calls it an early visual planning tool. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board)

### 10. Rationale and annotations

Annotate the intended meaning, not the visible fact. For example, explain that an image signals warmth, or that a type treatment signals precision. Figma uses this exact form of explanation in its commentary guidance. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)

Keep annotations short. Milanote says that notes, labels, or captions can provide enough context. [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

## Conditional extensions

These sections belong on the board when the product needs them. They are not required for every direction.

| Extension | Include it when | What it communicates | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motion | The product has animated, interactive, video, or spatial behavior | Pace, easing character, transition style, responsiveness, and emotional energy | Milanote recommends video and animation for projects with interactive or motion elements. Atlassian says that motion clarifies change, guides attention, and expresses brand. [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/motion) |
| Sound | Sound is part of the product or brand experience | Sonic mood, tempo, texture, and the relationship between sound and motion | Milanote includes audio as a digital moodboard element for a more immersive expression. [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) |
| Existing brand inheritance | An existing identity or design system constrains the direction | Which logos, colors, type, imagery, or components are fixed inputs | Milanote starts from existing brand values, statements, logos, and brand expressions. Adobe says that brand moodboards commonly include logos, color, slogans, and audience imagery. [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) |
| Illustration system | Illustration carries product meaning or brand expression | Subject, metaphor, detail, perspective, color, and where illustration appears in the product | Atlassian says that illustration can explain workflows, show context, guide tasks, and add brand personality. [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/guidelines/brand/illustrations/) |
| Data visualization | Charts or data are central to the product | Density, mark style, palette behavior, labels, and the balance between precision and expression | Atlassian lists grid, color, type, spacing, borders, and elevation as foundations that structure UI. This extension is an inference for data-heavy products. [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations) |

## Composition of the artifact itself

Use the board as evidence of its own direction. Figma says that a good board has rhythm and connects brand feeling to product function. Milanote says that composition, size, and position show importance and relationships. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

Use this visual order:

1. Put the direction header at the entry point.
2. Give the hero reference the greatest visual weight.
3. Group related evidence into visible clusters.
4. Place color, type, shape, material, and product fragments near references that explain them.
5. Keep annotations beside the item that they explain.
6. Reserve a clear area for feedback and approval.

This order follows Figma's recommendations for a central hero, grouped supporting elements, section labels, hierarchy, and feedback. It also follows Milanote's recommendations for composition, captions, notes, and collaboration. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

Aim for 10 to 15 high-impact images across one board. Figma gives this range and warns that too many images hide the intended look and feel. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)

Do not use one rigid grid for every direction. Figma and Milanote both treat the board's arrangement, rhythm, overlap, crop, and hierarchy as part of its expression. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

## Quality criteria

A professional board passes these tests:

- It is easy to understand at a glance. Figma names this as a property of a good moodboard. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)
- It aligns with a stated creative goal and audience. Figma and Adobe both start from the goal. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board)
- It has one clear visual hierarchy and one dominant anchor. Figma and Milanote both recommend this structure. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)
- It contains only references that support the same direction. Adobe says to remove unrelated styles and avoid overload. [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board)
- It explains why key references belong. Figma, Milanote, and Adobe all require or recommend commentary. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/ultimate-guide-mood-boards)
- It connects brand feeling to product function. Figma names this connection as part of the board's flow. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/)
- It remains open to feedback and iteration. Figma calls this a property of a good board. Milanote describes the board as an evolving artifact. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

## Implications for Argo issue #1960

Each generated direction needs the same content coverage, but it needs a distinct composition. Figma treats rhythm and arrangement as part of the direction. Milanote uses composition to express importance and relationships. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

The three boards need visible differences in image world, type, color behavior, composition, shape, material, and product fragments. These are separate foundations in the moodboard and design-system sources. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations)

Names and accent colors alone cannot make the directions distinct. This follows from the wider set of visual foundations that Figma, Adobe, Canva, and Atlassian identify. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board) [Canva](https://www.canva.com/create/mood-boards/) [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations)

The exploration skill needs visible references and short rationale before it asks for approval. Figma and Milanote both connect commentary and collaboration to useful feedback. [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Milanote](https://milanote.com/guide/create-better-moodboards)

The foundation skill can later convert the approved direction into exact tokens, assets, and components. Atlassian defines design tokens as the source of truth for interface decisions. The moodboard sources define the earlier artifact as a tool for direction and alignment. [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/tokens/design-tokens/) [Figma](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/how-to-make-a-mood-board/) [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/ideas/mood-board)
