# An AI-assisted planning sidebar for Tickets: prior art and opportunities

Checked 2026-09-25. This note uses first-party product documentation and Argo's own domain and architecture records. **Observed** statements describe documented product behavior. **Inference** statements are design proposals for Argo, not features those products have proved effective for Argo's users.

## Argo constraints

Argo distinguishes **Ticket** (provider-owned intent), **Session** (agent work), and **Delivery** (branch-keyed code and review state). These are linked but must not become one ranked hybrid entity. Ticket content remains provider-authoritative; Argo can link Sessions and Deliveries to Tickets. [Domain model](../domain/l1-organisation-entities.md), [relationships](../domain/relationships.md), [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-three-entity-domain-model.md).

Argo already records the product intent behind “what next”: provider priority supplies a sort, while Argo's recommendation is a *judgment* over blockers, completion, and sequencing, with a visible reason. It must degrade honestly when dependency data is missing. [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-design-target-fully-wired.md). A prior backlog-question design answers from the same listing for every reader—key, title, status, type, priority, labels, and `blockedBy`—rather than opportunistically using the body of tickets someone happened to open. [ADR-0031](../adr/0031-the-backlog-question-runs-on-codex-exec.md). Every displayed fact has an honesty tier; ambiguous observation resolves toward a quieter state rather than a false direct claim. [Honesty tier](../domain/honesty-tier.md).

## Opportunity matrix for a freed Ticket sidebar

These are hypotheses to prototype, ordered by likely usefulness in Argo's current model. They are not measured user findings.

| Sidebar module | One-line value | Inputs and links | Primary action | Failure mode to design for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Next move** | Show one or two ready Tickets with a concise “why now” and “what this unblocks.” | Ticket state, priority, labels, hierarchy, and `blockedBy`. [Argo Ticket model](../domain/l1-organisation-entities.md), [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-design-target-fully-wired.md). | Open Ticket, inspect rationale, or dismiss recommendation. | A confident ranking built from absent or stale dependency data; show the missing evidence or decline to rank. [Honesty tier](../domain/honesty-tier.md). |
| **Unlock map** | Identify the smallest blocker or decision whose resolution releases the most downstream work. | Verified `blockedBy` edges, blocker states, and Ticket hierarchy. [Argo Ticket model](../domain/l1-organisation-entities.md). | Open the blocking Ticket or filter the main list to the dependency chain. | Treating a ruled-out, unknown, or stale blocker as satisfied; show verification state per edge. |
| **Planning gaps** | State what is missing before work can start: reproduction, acceptance criteria, decision, dependency, or scope boundary. | Ticket title, body, labels, hierarchy, blockers, and provider fields available to every reader. [ADR-0031](../adr/0031-the-backlog-question-runs-on-codex-exec.md). | Draft a planning amendment for review or open the relevant source field. | Turning an AI preference into a mandatory process; identify concrete missing evidence and keep the Ticket provider authoritative. |
| **Work breakdown** | Propose a small ordered set of child Tickets or milestones for work that is too broad. | Ticket intent, existing hierarchy, dependencies, and related Ticket context. [Argo Ticket model](../domain/l1-organisation-entities.md). | Review a proposed breakdown before creating provider Tickets. | Inventing a second hidden plan or duplicating existing children; proposals must show overlap and create nothing without confirmation. |
| **Change surface** | Show which product domains or code areas are likely involved and why. | Ticket language plus Atlas Plots, Domains, Notes, and Couplings. [Argo Atlas model](../domain/atlas.md). | Inspect the affected area in Atlas or use it to scope implementation. | Presenting an inferred code area as certainty; label inference and show the files or historical coupling behind it. |
| **Related intent** | Surface possible duplicates, precedents, decisions, or Tickets that should be planned together. | Ticket index, labels, hierarchy, decision-ticket answers, and explicit relationships. [Argo Ticket model](../domain/l1-organisation-entities.md). | Compare Tickets, add an explicit relationship, or dismiss the suggestion. | Semantic similarity presented as a real relationship; keep suggestions separate until confirmed. |
| **Verification plan** | Turn acceptance criteria and risk into a compact checklist of evidence needed before the work is considered complete. | Ticket intent, labels, provider type, linked Delivery Diff and Checks when they exist. [Argo relationships](../domain/relationships.md). | Review the plan or open the exact check/evidence surface. | A generated checklist silently claiming completion; distinguish planned evidence from observed Check results. |
| **Scope alignment** | Compare the Ticket's stated intent with its linked Delivery and flag missing requirements or unrelated change. | Ticket intent plus linked Delivery Diff, Checks, Reviews, and Findings. [Argo relationships](../domain/relationships.md). | Inspect the mismatch in the main review surface. | Treating a language-model comparison as a verdict; cite exact Ticket text and changed paths, and label the conclusion as inferred. |
| **Ticket changes** | On return, summarize only planning-relevant changes: status, scope, dependencies, labels, hierarchy, or acceptance text. | Provider Ticket revisions and a recorded last-view time. Compare [Linear update drafting](https://linear.app/docs/initiative-and-project-updates). | Open the changed field or revision source. | Becoming a generic activity feed; exclude Session activity and changes that do not alter planning or execution. |

## Suggested composition

**Inference:** Use a *planning rail* whose modules change with the main route. On the Ticket list, show **Next move**, **Unlock map**, and **Planning gaps**. On a Ticket detail, show **Work breakdown**, **Change surface**, **Verification plan**, and **Scope alignment** when a Delivery exists. The rail contains Ticket intent and the planning and execution of that intent; Session attention, permissions, questions, and agent activity remain in Sessions. This uses the extra sidebar width for planning judgment while the main area gets full-width, readable Ticket rows. The proposal preserves Argo's separate Ticket, Session, and Delivery entities and its already specified next-work judgment. [Argo relationships](../domain/relationships.md), [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-design-target-fully-wired.md).

**Inference:** Make every recommendation inspectable: visible source links, reason, freshness, and a manual dismiss or correction path. Hold any generated prose to a narrower role than the source facts, especially when provider data is incomplete. This follows Argo's honesty-tier rule and the documented rationale/approval controls above. [Honesty tier](../domain/honesty-tier.md), [Linear Triage Intelligence](https://linear.app/docs/triage-intelligence), [GitHub rationale and approvals](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/concepts/agents/cloud-agent/about-automation-rationale-and-approvals).
