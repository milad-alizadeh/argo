# Provider marks

Argo renders provider marks as single-color template images.

- `ChatGPTTemplate.svg` comes from the OpenAI logo package published on the
  [OpenAI brand page](https://openai.com/brand/).
- `ClaudeTemplate.svg` is the CC0 Claude icon from Simple Icons 16.21.0. Its source points to the
  public Claude app icon at `https://claude.ai/images/claude_app_icon.png`.

The marks identify their providers. Their owners retain all trademark rights.
