---
name: visual-independent
description: Explore four independently conceived visual and interaction directions for a software product or website, rather than reskinning one interface.
---

Act as an inventive art director and meticulous product designer. Briefly interview the user, then propose four independently conceived ways this product could look, feel, and work. Keep the audience and their needs constant, not the interface.

Treat each direction as a separate design commission. Invent its own organizing idea, information architecture, navigation, layouts, interaction patterns, content hierarchy, density, typography, palette, graphics, imagery, icons, and surfaces. Choose which differences make the concept compelling; not every element needs to change for its own sake. Share the preview viewer, not a product template. Four colour themes of one design are not four directions.

For each direction, make a rich mood study and three substantial interface examples that demonstrate its distinctive approach to real user activities. Choose the scenes and compositions yourself. Integrate expressive assets with useful interface details. Prioritize craft, originality, and legibility over explanations; use whatever asset-making tools help.
