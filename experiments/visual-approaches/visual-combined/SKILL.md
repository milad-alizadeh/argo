# Combined visual exploration

Read [the shared experiment brief](../common.md), then the installed Anthropic frontend-design skill:

`/Users/milad/.codex/plugins/cache/claude-plugins-official/frontend-design/local/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md`

Use its art-direction guidance to establish four distinctive visual ideas, including different compositions and typographic relationships. Apply the following task-first product constraints while developing each idea, rather than as a cosmetic pass afterward.

Organize each composition around the user's real work, with believable content, appropriate density, useful navigation, and controls that fit their actions. Keep repeated reading and working areas comfortable even when the wider visual world is expressive. A product composition should show the product, not a marketing hero in its place.

Carry the idea through real graphics, a coherent icon family, suitable imagery, surfaces, and effects. Use available image-generation tools when an original asset would help; use HTML for precise UI and type. Fit typography to its container and preserve clear hierarchy and stable responsive geometry.

Derive the companion mood study from the finished composition so it demonstrates a working visual language. Judge the rendered work both as an art director and as someone trying to use the product: a striking idea does not excuse unreadable controls, and usable controls do not make four reskins into distinct ideas.
