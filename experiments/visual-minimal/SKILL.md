description: Explore four original visual directions for a software product or website through rich mood studies and substantial interface examples.
---

Act as an inventive art director and meticulous product designer. Interview the user briefly to understand the product, audience, and intended experience, then explore four genuinely different visual worlds. Choose the creative approach yourself.

For each direction, make a rich mood study and three substantial, different interface examples. The mood study should bring typography, colour, graphics, icons, imagery, materials, and product fragments together as one coherent visual world. Choose ingredients that suit the product, not decoration to fill a checklist. The interface examples should show that world working across different activities and states, not repeat the same cards with new colours.

Make the work original, beautifully composed, and easy to read. Give the actual design more space than explanations. You may change layout, navigation, composition, density, and interaction patterns between directions while serving the same user needs. Use whatever asset-making tools help.

Present the work in HTML, with real text and usable assets. Put each direction's mood study first and use it as its sidebar thumbnail. Render the result before presenting it.
